### Fixed

- `bin/bgrun-container-guard` (the LOW findings of the round-2 review of PR 291): the same forwarded signal again within 0.25 s is dropped, because `gtimeout` without `--foreground` signals the pid and the group, so one timeout could reach Apple's CLI three times (kernel + two forwards) and make it force-exit with the VM left behind; the CLI now hears at most two TERMs. Whether Ctrl-C reaches the command from the terminal is decided by the controlling terminal (`/dev/tty`, our group foreground), not by stdin: with stdin redirected inside a terminal (`echo x | bgrun container ...`, `< /dev/null`) each Ctrl-C used to reach the CLI twice. New tests: a pty Ctrl-C reaches the command exactly once with stdin as /dev/null, a pipe or the tty; INT is forwarded once with no controlling terminal or from a background group; a repeated TERM is forwarded once; a `gtimeout` TERM reaches the command at most twice. (#PR)
