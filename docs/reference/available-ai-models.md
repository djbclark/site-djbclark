## Important Notes / Caveats

1. **Plan / Account Restrictions.** Availability of specific models or higher
   thinking levels can be limited by the active plan (Codex Plus, Claude Pro,
   Cursor Pro, Google AI Pro, SuperGrok, OpenCode Go subscription, Zed
   Free/Pro, etc.). Some models (e.g. GPT-5.3-codex-spark, Claude Mythos,
   certain pro variants) are restricted to higher tiers.

2. **Dynamic Catalogs.** OpenRouter and Poe maintain large, frequently updated
   catalogs (200–300+ models). The rows above cover the major current families;
   the live lists at openrouter.ai/models and poe.com should be consulted for
   the absolute latest additions or removals.

3. **Thinking Level Semantics.**
   - "Adaptive Thinking", "Effort", "reasoning_effort", "Light/Medium/High/…",
     "Minimal/Low/Medium/High", and "Enabled/Disabled" are the terms used by the
     respective providers. Exact parameter names and defaults differ.
   - "N/A" is used for pure speech, image/video, or specialized edit-prediction
     models that do not expose text reasoning controls.

4. **Aliases & Deprecations.** DeepSeek `deepseek-chat` and `deepseek-reasoner`
   are scheduled for deprecation around 24 July 2026 and currently map to
   V4-Flash non-thinking / thinking modes respectively. Legacy Grok and Claude
   IDs may redirect to newer equivalents.

5. **Third-party / Local / BYOK.** Codex (via config.toml), Cursor (API pool),
   Zed (BYOK + local llama.cpp), OpenRouter, and Poe all allow bringing external
   models. In those cases the thinking levels are those of the underlying model.

6. **Verification Date.** All entries reflect publicly documented availability
   as of mid-July 2026. Always confirm in the official product UI or
   documentation for the most current status.

7. **Operator-verified corrections.** 2026-07-19: the Claude GUI exposes a
   selectable Low/Medium/High/Extra/Max/Ultra effort picker for Fable 5 on
   monthly plans (row 10 updated; no Auto option for effort). The lower-left
   yellow "Auto" control in that UI is a separate setting (safety/routing),
   not the thinking level — leave it alone. Other Claude-web rows may have
   the same picker; verify in-UI before relying on the listed levels.
